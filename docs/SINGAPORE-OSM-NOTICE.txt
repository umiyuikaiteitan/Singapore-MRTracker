Singapore MRT/LRT starter — reconstructed rail geometry

Track shapes are derived from OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors,
available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The accompanying singapore-mrt.zip includes these derived shapes in shapes.txt.
Original route and stop information comes from the LTA DataMall train GTFS feed.
Shapes supplied by LTA are preserved; only missing shapes are reconstructed.
The dated OSM snapshot and reconstruction coverage are recorded in
singapore-mrt-provenance.json. Unmatched patterns are explicitly listed there
and remain disclosed stop-to-stop fallbacks in the editor.

The reconstruction uses mapped active track topology and GTFS stop order. It is
an inferred alignment, not an authoritative statement of operational routing.
